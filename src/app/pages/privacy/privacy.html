Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy & Data Governance Policy

How Good Enterprises safeguards corporate requisition details, vendor documentation, and workforce biometric records.

Last Revised: {{ lastUpdated }} Compliance Standard: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023

1. Commitment to Client & Workforce Privacy

GOOD ENTERPRISES ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a professional industrial manpower service provider. We are dedicated to maintaining strict confidentiality and highest standards of data security for our prospective corporate clients, partner factories, and candidate workforce across Tamil Nadu.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Corporate Client Information: When you submit a manpower requisition, request a company profile, or contact us via our website, we collect your contact person name, company name, plant/factory location, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, candidate category requirements, and shift preferences.
2.2 Candidate & Worker Data: In our operational role as staffing employer, we collect verified candidate records including government ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID), educational marksheets, ITI/Diploma certificates, background verification clearance, emergency contacts, and bank account numbers solely for statutory PF/ESI enrollment and direct wage disbursement.

3. How We Use Collected Information

  • Preparing accurate staffing estimates, commercial rate cards, and customized manpower proposals
  • Fulfilling industrial staffing service level agreements (SLAs) and managing daily shift rosters
  • Processing statutory deposits with the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) and Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC)
  • Providing monthly wage registers, attendance logs, and audit files to client HR departments
  • Communicating operational shift updates and emergency replacement coordination

4. Non-Disclosure & Data Protection

We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease corporate client lists, factory operational data, or workforce personal information to any third-party marketing agencies. Data is restricted strictly to authorized staffing coordinators and compliance officers on a need-to-know basis.

5. Mandatory Disclosures to Statutory Authorities

We disclose records solely when required by law or judicial process to competent regulatory authorities, including:

  • Regional Provident Fund Commissioner (EPFO)
  • Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) inspection authorities
  • Office of the Labour Commissioner under the Contract Labour (CLRA) Act
  • Goods and Services Tax (GST) and Income Tax regulatory bodies

6. Security of Electronic Communications

All inquiries submitted through our web portal are transmitted over secure HTTPS encrypted channels. Requisition data is safeguarded in secure databases with access logging and multi-factor authentication.

7. Data Retention & Client Rights

Statutory labor records and payroll registers are retained for periods mandated under the Factories Act and Tamil Nadu Contract Labour Rules (minimum five years). Corporate clients may request updates or deletion of preliminary inquiry data at any time by contacting our compliance office.
