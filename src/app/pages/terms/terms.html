Legal & Contractual Terms

Terms of Service & Staffing Engagement

Standard commercial terms, service level commitments, and operational policies governing manpower deployment by Good Enterprises.

Effective Date: {{ lastUpdated }} Governing Law: State of Tamil Nadu, Republic of India

1. Scope of Staffing Services

GOOD ENTERPRISES ("the Service Provider") agrees to source, vet, deploy, and administer technical, skilled, semi-skilled, and general workforce personnel ("Deployed Personnel") to the designated factory, facility, or warehouse of the client company ("the Client") located within Tamil Nadu industrial corridors including Ranipet, Ambur, Vellore, Ambattur, Sriperumbudur, and Vanagaram.

2. Mobilization & Service Level Commitments (SLA)

2.1 Turnaround Time: Standard industrial profiles (helpers, machine operators, housekeeping) shall be mobilized within twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) hours of written requisition confirmation. Specialized engineering or ITI trades shall be mobilized within three (3) business days.
2.2 Attendance Guarantee: The Service Provider maintains a buffer reserve pool of 10–15% to guarantee a minimum shift attendance rate of 98.5%.
2.3 Candidate Replacement: If any deployed personnel is found unsuitable in trade capability or conduct within forty-eight (48) hours of reporting, the Client may request an immediate replacement at zero additional placement surcharge.

3. Statutory & Labor Law Compliance

The Service Provider warrants that it holds valid establishment codes and shall strictly adhere to:

  • Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 (12% EPF deposit)
  • Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 (3.25% ESIC contribution)
  • Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970 (Form V & licensing)
  • Payment of Minimum Wages Act (Tamil Nadu Gazette schedule revisions)
  • Workmen's Compensation Insurance coverage for on-site industrial hazards

Copies of monthly ECR challans, wage registers, and biometric attendance logs shall accompany each billing invoice.

4. Workplace Safety & Client Responsibilities

The Client shall ensure that all plant machinery, tools, and industrial floor conditions comply with the Factories Act, 1948. The Client shall conduct initial machine safety briefings and provide specialized line Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) where required, while standard safety gear is verified by Good Enterprises prior to deployment.

5. Commercial Invoicing & Payment Terms

5.1 Invoicing Cycle: Invoices shall be raised on a monthly calendar basis supported by signed muster rolls and biometric attendance records.
5.2 Statutory Taxes: Applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST 18%) shall be charged as per Government of India notifications.
5.3 Payment Window: The Client shall remit invoiced amounts via electronic bank transfer within the agreed credit window (strictly 15 to 30 days from date of bill submission).

6. Confidentiality & Non-Solicitation

Both parties agree to treat proprietary plant processes, shift schedules, and operational data as strictly confidential. The Client agrees not to directly employ deployed personnel on permanent company rolls within six (6) months of contract completion without prior written consent and placement settlement.

7. Jurisdiction & Dispute Resolution

Any dispute or divergence arising from or connected with this agreement shall first be resolved through good-faith executive mediation. In the event of unresolved disputes, the exclusive legal jurisdiction shall reside in the competent courts of Vellore / Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
